Frequently asked

Questions about OLVM.

What is OLVM?

OLVM is the Solana-based ecosystem token of OLIVIUM. It is designed to connect the digital community with the developing OLIVIUM ecosystem, including Guardian opportunities and estate experiences under published terms.

How are Guardian tiers determined?

Guardian tiers are determined by qualifying OLVM holdings. The current published thresholds are 50,000 OLVM for Guardian, 500,000 OLVM for Heritage Guardian, 2,500,000 OLVM for Estate Guardian and 10,000,000 OLVM for Founder.

Do I need to spend a certain amount of money to become a Guardian?

Guardian eligibility is defined by OLVM holdings, not by a euro or dollar purchase amount. The relevant requirement is the qualifying OLVM balance in the applicable wallet, according to the published program rules.

Does OLVM mean I own part of the estate?

No. Holding OLVM does not transfer ownership of the land, villa, company, olive trees or business. Guardian participation and estate benefits are separate from legal ownership.

Does OLVM guarantee profits or returns?

No. OLVM should not be represented as providing guaranteed profits, dividends, yield or price appreciation.

What is a Heritage Guardian?

Heritage Guardian is the 500,000 OLVM tier. Subject to the published Guardian terms, this tier is intended to provide a deeper relationship with the grove, including a specific Guardian Tree and defined estate experiences.

Will the trees have digital identities?

The planned OLIVIUM tree layer is designed to give individual trees digital identities connected to physical grove information, imagery and seasonal history. Final implementation details will be published separately.

Where is OLVM launching?

OLVM is planned for launch on Solana through Pump.fun. The direct official launch link and verified token address will be published once the token is live.
